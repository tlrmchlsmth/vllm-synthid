methodology

Each result measures sustained synthetic vLLM EngineCore data-plane throughput. An out-of-tree scheduler plugin sends deterministic SchedulerOutputs through EngineCore, the real executor, and workers with a bounded internal pipeline. Resident KV histories are constructed before measurement. Measurement details are in report.json. Immutable artifact hashes are in artifact-manifest.json.
